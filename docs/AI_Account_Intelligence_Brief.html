A human-approved multi-agent workflow that researches target accounts, scores ICP fit, drafts source-grounded outreach, and routes qualified cases to Slack review before CRM sync. It shows how AI agents can improve a business workflow without removing human judgment.

Product Spec

Input: target company list and ICP profile.
Core: CrewAI sequential agents, Tavily source extraction, validation harness, PostgreSQL state.
Output: structured JSON, fit rationale, outreach draft, status history, and reviewable Slack card.

Why It Matters

Teams lose hours researching accounts, judging fit, and writing first drafts. This system turns that manual loop into a governed workflow: faster research, clearer evidence, reusable ICP logic, and human approval before action.

Control Model

The AI is not allowed to become an unsupervised sales bot. It uses public company data only, keeps source URLs, logs every run, flags uncertainty, and sends qualified drafts to human review before any CRM movement.
