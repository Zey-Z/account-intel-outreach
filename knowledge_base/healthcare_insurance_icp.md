# Healthcare / Insurance ICP Playbook

Use this playbook only for interpreting target accounts and choosing outreach
angles. Do not use it as evidence about a target company.

Strong fit signals:
- Claims, prior authorization, eligibility, benefits, provider network, payer operations, or member support workflow language.
- Hiring or product updates around operations, support, document intake, or workflow automation.
- Public messaging that shows queue-heavy or evidence-heavy business processes.

Disqualifiers:
- Direct patient diagnosis or treatment workflow.
- Any request that would require PHI in this portfolio demo.
- Consumer wellness content with no operations workflow.
